- **Separate only content that's genuinely different.** Dividers earn their place between sections that are clearly distinct. Too many make an interface look cluttered and broken up, so check whether whitespace would group the content just as well.
- **Scale the weight to the break.** Let a divider's prominence reflect how important the separation is: a major section break can take `color="strong"`, while a minor grouping needs a subtler line or just space.
- **Follow the layout's direction.** Set `direction` so the line runs across the flow of the content it separates, between items stacked vertically or between items laid out side by side.
- **Try spacing first.** Spacing props on your layout components often group content more cleanly than a line. Add a divider to make things clearer, not as a substitute for well-planned spacing.

See [Shopify's best practices for `<s-divider>`](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/app-home/latest/web-components/layout-and-structure/divider#best-practices) for the full guidance.
